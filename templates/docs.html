What the arena shows (and refuses to show)

    
Live views render one agent's local truth: its own
    position, its barrier knowledge, the scent field it perceives, the hints it received, and its
    BELIEF hologram over the opponent — never the opponent's true position.
    The full bird's-eye exists only in the replay cinema of settled
    games, rebuilt from the mutually revealed audit trails with per-step
    Verified OK stamps. That is not a style choice; it is the rulebook.
